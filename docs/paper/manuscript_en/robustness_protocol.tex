\subsection{Frozen-Policy Robustness Evaluation}
\hl{Two frozen Full policies (training seeds 11001 and 11002) were tested at the strict $1$ mm/$0.025$ rad tolerance, without retraining. Each policy was evaluated on 1000 episodes per condition, retaining the 500-step horizon and five-consecutive-sample success criterion. At each 20-Hz control cycle, position measurements received independent, zero-mean Gaussian translation noise with per-axis standard deviation $\sigma_p$; orientation measurements were perturbed separately by left-multiplicative SO(3) rotations with independent Gaussian rotation-vector components of standard deviation $\sigma_R$. The noisy pose consistently determined pose/keypoint features and precision-dependent action scaling. Joint states, velocities, and model-derived Jacobians remained exact. Success was determined from the true simulated state.}

\hl{Mechanical disturbances were world-frame forces of fixed magnitude (0, 5, 10, or 20 N), applied at the current tool origin throughout each episode and reapplied at every 240-Hz physics substep. Force directions were sampled uniformly on the sphere every 0.25 s. Measurements remained exact in the force-only sweep. The combined sweep used 20 N with position noise and exact orientation measurements. Tasks and perturbation sequences were paired across conditions within each sweep, using random streams independent of task sampling. Single-perturbation evaluations used test-seed block 41001; the combined sweep used block 51001, separate from pilot tasks.}
