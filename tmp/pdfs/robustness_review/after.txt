   This paper presented an SAC framework for local con-
vergence enhancement under strict position–orientation
constraints. The proposed framework raises success at 10
mm/0.10 rad from KP-SAC-Base’s 84.75% to 96.35% and
reaches 1 mm/0.025 rad. Without Boundary Replay, M2
achieves 89.23% at this strict tolerance; without Jacobian
features, M3 reaches only 1 mm/0.10 rad with 77.13% suc-
cess. No clear independent gain from Bottleneck shaping
is observed.
